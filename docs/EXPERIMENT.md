# EXPERIMENT - result_view

> **Synthetic data:** The collected dataset is primarily synthetic (`sim-` identities in Flagsmith, `synthetic=1` in SQLite and CSV exports), generated by `quiz/simulate.py` from fixed documented parameters (`commit 69b712a`). It shows that Verdict works end-to-end on this Flagsmith pipeline, and is **not** evidence about human behaviour. Real-only counts and verdicts are always shown alongside combined totals.

## Question
Does a verdict memo, compared with a standard win-probability view, reduce the share of people who choose to ship an inconclusive result?

## Hypothesis (pre-registered before collecting data; commit this file before sharing the link)
H1: `verdict` lowers `shipped_ambiguous` relative to `dashboard`.
Assumed baseline for control: about 60% (assumption, to be replaced by measured control rate).
Minimum effect of interest: 20 percentage points.

## Design
- Variants: `dashboard` (control) vs `verdict`, 50/50, assigned by Flagsmith to a persistent visitor identity.
- Exposure: scenario 1 render.
- Primary metric: `shipped_ambiguous` (lower is better).
- Guardrail: `held_clear_win` (attached to experiment `2` in Flagsmith with `expected_direction: not_increase`, and also logged in SQLite as a cross-check; must not rise materially; scenario 2 is a genuinely strong result).
- Planned sample: target at least 200 real completions (about 100 per arm), minimum 100 for a credible reading; fewer is reported honestly as a limitation. Decision time fixed in advance: **Nov 29, 2026, 09:00 IST**. Do not stop early because the result looks good.
- Unit of analysis: visitor. One decision per scenario per visitor.

## Scenarios (fictional numbers)
- S1 (ambiguous): variant +11.2% lift, 86% probability to beat control, interval -9.8% to +34.1%, 140 users per arm of a planned 800. Correct: HOLD.
- S2 (clear): variant +18.4% lift, 99% probability, interval +9.1% to +27.7%, planned sample reached, split matches the allocation. Correct: SHIP.

## Quality controls
- Stable identity; persisted cookie; exposure de-duplicated.
- Exposures in both arms; exposure counts compared with SQLite log.
- SRM check on the experiment's own split.
- Simulated traffic excluded from the headline result and reported separately.

## Known limitations (state in the final report)
- Respondents are mostly developers recruited from hackathon/social channels (selection bias).
- Scenarios are fixed and fictional; effect on real decisions may differ.
- Short collection window; intervals may be wide.
- The tested tool is the one evaluating the experiment (conflict of interest); the policy is deterministic and published.

Verdict outputs and the results page will always report combined N, real N, synthetic N, the **combined verdict**, and the **real-only verdict** side by side (and flag any disagreement explicitly).
- **2026-11-26 (pre-run generative model lock, commit `69b712a`):** `quiz/simulate.py` is committed at `69b712a` (`69b712a170c0502fd5d01c5dc0d454b8b39961f3`) before sending any synthetic events and will never be tuned after observing results. Parameters and rationale:
  - **Primary (`s1` `shipped_ambiguous`):** `P(ship | dashboard) = 0.60`, `P(ship | verdict) = 0.42`. Rationale: tests an 18 pp reduction close to the pre-registered 20 pp MDE rather than an easy best-case separation, so a 200-visitor run tests Verdict under realistic uncertainty.
  - **Guardrail (`s2` `held_clear_win`):** `P(hold | dashboard) = 0.08`, `P(hold | verdict) = 0.09`. Rationale: reflects small false-caution noise on a genuinely strong win with no material guardrail regression.
  - **Completion rate:** `0.88` equal in both arms (12% deterministic post-exposure drop-off after `/s1` from fixed seed `20261126`). Rationale: models realistic funnel attrition between `/s1` exposure and `/done` without differential attrition or SRM.
  - **Synthetic volume (`--count` default):** `max(0, 200 - real_exposures)` read from SQLite (`synthetic=0`). Rationale: tops total exposures cleanly to the pre-registered `200` planned sample without exceeding it when real participants already completed the quiz.
- **2026-11-26 (Experiment 2 `started_at` SQLite window filter):** The untouched pre-Experiment-2 phone-test row (`sim-466fd99f-5c0d-4045-a4ac-3c3d228ed10a`, recorded at `2026-11-26T06:14:56.758757+00:00` during Experiment 1) remains preserved in SQLite (`data/quiz.db`) for auditability.
